1

The hook: YOU は SHOCK

0:06–0:17

Two lines, same shape. Listen for the English word after は, and for the verb 落ちてくる at the end of each line.

wordmeaning
YOU は SHOCKEnglish noun + は = a Japanese sentence. 80s J-pop loved this. "You (are) shock" = you shock me.
愛あいlove
空そらsky
胸むねchest — and in songs, the heart
落おちてくる落ちる (fall) + てくる = falls toward me
Verb-て + くる = the action comes toward the speaker. Verb-て + いく = it goes away. The sky falls onto me: 落ちてくる.
  • 雨あめが降ふってきた it started to rain (on me)
  • 友ともだちが走はしってくる my friend comes running (toward me)
  • 鳥とりが飛とんでいく the bird flies away

Drill

  1. バスが！The bus came (here)! (来る, past)
  2. 雪ゆきが降ふって。It started snowing. (てくる, past)
  3. 電車でんしゃが行いって。The train went away (and I missed it). (てしまう)
2

Verse: chains are useless

0:18–0:29

Fast and rough. Listen for 〜ても (even if) followed by 無駄, and the word ダウン at the very end.

wordmeaning
熱あつい心こころa burning heart (熱い = hot, passionate)
鎖くさりchain
つなぐto tie, to chain up (つないでも = even if you chain it)
無駄むだuseless, no use — one of the most useful words in Japan
邪魔じゃまするto get in the way (邪魔する奴 = anyone who gets in the way)
奴やつguy, fellow — rough. Fine for villains, not for coworkers.
指先ゆびさきひとつでwith a single fingertip (N + ひとつで = with just one N)
ダウンdown (a knockdown) — loanwords work as nouns: ダウンさ = they go down
Verb-て + も = even if. The verse says: even if you chain a burning heart, it's useless. 〜ても無駄だ is a set phrase worth stealing.
  • 今いま謝あやまっても無駄むだだ apologising now is useless
  • 雨あめが降ふっても行いく I'll go even if it rains
  • ボタンひとつで注文ちゅうもんできる you can order with a single button

Drill

  1. 高たかく買かう。I'll buy it even if it's expensive. (い-adj: 高く + ても)
  2. 何回なんかい説明せつめいし。Explaining it any number of times is useless.
  3. スマホ旅行りょこうできる。You can travel with just a smartphone.
3

Hook again: heartbeat gets faster

0:30–0:41

Same hook, new ending. Listen for 鼓動 and the change verb 速くなる.

wordmeaning
鼓動こどうheartbeat (鼓 = drum, 動 = move)
速はやい → 速はやくなるfast → become fast. い-adjective: drop い, add くなる
俺おれ"I" — rough, masculine. Kenshiro's pronoun. Use 私 with strangers.
い-adj → 〜くなる / な-adj, noun → 〜になる = to become. 速くなる, 静かになる, 医者になる.
  • 日本語にほんごが上手じょうずになった my Japanese got good
  • 夜よるは寒さむくなる it gets cold at night
  • 鼓動こどうが速はやくなる my heartbeat speeds up

Drill

  1. 暑あつい → 暑あつhot → get hot
  2. 静しずか → 静しずかquiet → become quiet
  3. 大人おとな → 大人おとなadult → become an adult
4

Build-up: a wandering heart

0:41–0:53

The tension part. Big literary verbs, and it ends on 〜はずさ.

wordmeaning
求もとめるto seek, to long for (愛を求める = seek love)
彷徨さまようto wander — a poetic word, huge in songs
燃もえるto burn (熱く燃えてる = is burning hot)
溶とかすto melt (something) — transitive. 溶ける = melts by itself
無残むざんにcruelly, without mercy — pure 北斗 vocabulary
飛とび散ちるto scatter, to fly apart (飛ぶ + 散る)
〜はずさit is bound to ~ / it should ~. さ = a cool, masculine sentence-ender
Plain form + はずだ = I'm confident it's so (based on reason). Not a wish: an expectation. In the song: it's bound to melt everything and scatter.
  • もう着ついているはずだ he should have arrived by now
  • 今日きょうは休やすみのはずだ today should be a day off
  • そんなはずはない that can't be right

Drill

  1. メールを送おくったから、届とどいている。I sent the mail, so it should have arrived.
  2. アイスが暑あつさで。The ice cream melted from the heat. (intransitive 溶ける)
  3. チョコをケーキに入いれる。Melt the chocolate and put it in the cake. (transitive 溶かす)
5

Chorus: take back the love

0:55–1:18

The chorus. Listen for 〜ため (in order to), 見失った, and the command at the very end: 取り戻せ.

wordmeaning
守まもるto protect
〜ため(に)in order to (守るため = in order to protect)
旅立たびだつto set out on a journey (旅 + 立つ)
明日あすtomorrow — read あす here, the formal/poetic reading (everyday: あした)
見失みうしなうto lose sight of (見る + 失う)
微笑ほほえみa smile (noun from 微笑む)
〜など"things like ~" — here it's dismissive: a face like that? no thanks
見みたくはないI do NOT want to see (見たい → 見たく + は + ない, は adds stress)
取とり戻もどす → 取とり戻もどせto get back → GET IT BACK! (imperative)
Imperative form (命令形): う-verbs → change the last u to e (取り戻す → 取り戻せ, 行く → 行け). る-verbs → ろ (食べる → 食べろ). する → しろ, 来る → 来い. Rough and direct: for songs, sports, emergencies, and Kenshiro.
  • 頑張がんばれ！ Go for it! (the one imperative everyone uses)
  • 逃にげろ！ Run!
  • 愛あいを取とり戻もどせ！ Take back the love!

Drill — make it an order

  1. 行いく → go → Go!
  2. 食たべる → eat → Eat!
  3. する → do → Do it! (irregular)
  4. 来くる → come → Come! (irregular, こい)
  5. 日本語にほんごを勉強べんきょうする東京とうきょうに来きた。I came to Tokyo in order to study Japanese.

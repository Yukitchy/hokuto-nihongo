Kanji trick: 強敵 = とも

強敵きょうてき normally means "powerful enemy" (きょうてき). In Hokuto no Ken it is read とも = "friend". A rival you respect is a friend. Japanese does this all the time: Xと書いてYと読む (written X, read Y).

  • 本気ほんきと書いて「マジ」と読む written "serious", read "for real"
  • 宇宙うちゅうと書いて「そら」と読む written "universe", read "sky"

1

お前はもう死んでいる N4→N3 · 〜ている

Kenshiro · the line everyone knows

お前おまえはもう死しんでいる。

Omae wa mō shinde iru.

"You are already dead."

Clip
Listen & repeat
Record yourself
Clip: YouTube · bonus at 0:08 — the famous death cry ひでぶ！

Word by word

wordmeaning
お前おまえyou — rough. For enemies, rivals, very close friends. Never for your boss or your host family.
はtopic marker (wa)
もうalready
死しんでいる死ぬ → て-form 死んで + いる → "is (in the state of) dead"

Grammar: 〜ている = the state after a change

change verb + ている → not "is doing", but "has done and is now in that state".

死ぬ, 結婚する, 知る, 持つ, 住む, 始まる, 閉まる are all "change" verbs. Kenshiro is not saying "you are dying". The change already happened; you just haven't noticed.

  • もう結婚けっこんしている I'm (already) married
  • 電車でんしゃはもう出でている the train has already left
  • 店みせはもう閉しまっている the shop is already closed
Tokyo use: a friend runs for the last train — you say「もう出てるよ」(colloquial drops the い).

Drill

  1. 会議かいぎはもう。The meeting has already started. (始まる)
  2. すみません、お金かねをんです。I don't have money on me. (持つ, negative)
  3. 今いまは東京とうきょうに。I live in Tokyo now. (住む)

我が生涯に一片の悔いなし！ N3→N2 · 我が / 〜なし

Raoh · his last words, fist raised to the sky

我わが生涯しょうがいに一片いっぺんの悔くいなし！

Waga shōgai ni ippen no kui nashi!

"In my life, not one piece of regret!"

Clip
Listen & repeat
Record yourself
Clip: YouTube

Word by word

wordmeaning
我わがmy / our — literary 私の. Still alive in 我が国 (our country), 我が社 (our company), 我が家 (our home)
生涯しょうがい(whole) life, lifetime
にin
一片いっぺんのone piece of / a single
悔くいregret (noun of 悔やむ)
なし= ない. Literary "there is none"

Grammar: 〜なし — the sign-board negative

Noun + なし = "no ~ / without ~". Short, hard, final. Used on signs, reports, and by dying warlords.
  • 問題もんだいなし no problem (report / text)
  • 異常いじょうなし nothing abnormal (health check)
  • 予約よやくなしで入はいれます you can enter without a reservation
  • 我わが家やへようこそ welcome to our home
Tokyo use: a restaurant sign 「予約なしOK」. A LINE reply from your boss: 「問題なし」= all good.

Drill

  1. テストの結果けっかは？ — ！No problem!
  2. 健康診断けんこうしんだん：。Health check: nothing abnormal.
  3. ようこそ、へ！Welcome to our home! (我が + 家)
Raise your fist when you say it.
3

退かぬ！媚びぬ！省みぬ！ N2 · classical 〜ぬ

Souther, the Holy Emperor · three vows in three words

退ひかぬ！媚こびぬ！省かえりみぬ！

Hikanu! Kobinu! Kaeriminu!

"I won't retreat! I won't flatter! I won't look back!"

Clip
Listen & repeat
Record yourself
Clip: YouTube

Word by word

verbmeaning〜ぬ form
退ひくto step back, retreat退かぬ
媚こびるto flatter, suck up to媚びぬ
省かえりみるto look back on, reflect省みぬ
Rule: take the ない-stem and swap ない → ぬ. 行かない → 行かぬ. する → せぬ. 来る → 来(こ)ぬ.

Grammar: 〜ぬ = old-school ない

〜ぬ is the classical negative. It sounds samurai, dramatic, absolute. You will hear it in anime, proverbs, and a few fixed phrases everyone still uses.
  • 知しらぬ間まに before I knew it
  • 見知みしらぬ人ひと a stranger (an un-known person)
  • 思おもわぬプレゼント an unexpected present
  • 言いわぬが花はな some things are better left unsaid (proverb)
Tokyo use: 見知らぬ人 shows up in news and station announcements. 知らぬ間に is everyday speech.

Drill — convert to 〜ぬ

  1. 行いかない → won't go
  2. 食たべない → won't eat
  3. 諦あきらめない → won't give up
  4. しない → won't do (irregular!)
Shout it. Souther would.

汚物は消毒だ〜！ N3 · XはYだ / 〜べきだ

a mohawk with a flamethrower · the most quoted mob line in Japan

汚物おぶつは消毒しょうどくだ〜！

Obutsu wa shōdoku da!

"Filth gets disinfected!"

Clip
Listen & repeat
Record yourself
Clip: YouTube · at 0:19 Kenshiro answers: 汚物は消毒すべきだな

Word by word

wordmeaning
汚物おぶつfilth, waste (汚 = dirty, 物 = thing)
はtopic
消毒しょうどくdisinfection — a する-noun (消毒する)
だ"is" — plain copula, said with full confidence
Literally "filth is disinfection". Japanese lets a noun sentence mean "X → the treatment is Y". Very common in speech.

Grammar: XはYだ！ as a battle cry · 〜べきだ = should

Noun は Noun だ！ — announce the plan. Verb(dictionary) + べきだ — "should / ought to". Kenshiro replies「消毒すべきだな」(する → すべき).
  • 今日きょうは焼肉やきにくだ！ Today = yakiniku!
  • 週末しゅうまつは大掃除おおそうじだ！ The weekend = big cleaning!
  • 手ては消毒しょうどくすべきだ you should disinfect your hands
  • 早はやく寝ねるべきだ you should go to bed early
Tokyo use: 消毒 is on every shop entrance bottle since 2020: 「手指しゅし消毒にご協力ください」.

Drill

  1. 風邪かぜを引ひいたら、。If you catch a cold, you should rest. (休む)
  2. 傷口きずぐちはしてください。Please disinfect the wound.
  3. 今夜こんやは！Tonight = ramen!
Extend the だ〜 for full effect.
5

愛や情けは悲しみしか生まぬ N3 · 〜しか〜ない / 〜み

Souther, dying · the question that made a generation cry

愛あいや情なさけは悲かなしみしか生うまぬ。

Ai ya nasake wa kanashimi shika umanu.

"Love and mercy give birth to nothing but sorrow."

Clip
Listen & repeat
Record yourself
Clip: YouTube · then: なのに、なぜ悲しみを背負おうとする？ — "so why do you choose to carry sorrow?"

Word by word

wordmeaning
愛あいlove
や"and (things like)" — an open list. AやB = A, B, and so on
情なさけmercy, compassion, human warmth
悲かなしみsorrow — noun made from 悲しい
しか 〜 ないonly / nothing but (always with a negative)
生うまぬ生む (give birth, produce) + ぬ = 生まない

Grammar: 〜しか〜ない · い-adjective → 〜み noun

Noun + しか + negative = only ~, nothing but ~. Feels more "that's all there is" than だけ.
悲しい → 悲しみ, 苦しい → 苦しみ, 楽しい → 楽しみ, 痛い → 痛み. Drop い, add み = the feeling as a noun.
  • 千円せんえんしかない I only have 1,000 yen
  • 日本語にほんごしか話はなせない I can only speak Japanese
  • 週末しゅうまつが楽たのしみだ I'm looking forward to the weekend
Tokyo use: 「現金しか使えません」cash only. 「楽しみにしています」= looking forward to it (say this to Yuuki after class).

Drill

  1. 財布さいふに百円ひゃくえんない。I only have 100 yen in my wallet.
  2. この店みせは現金げんきん。This shop only takes cash. (使う, potential negative)
  3. 楽たのしい → fun → something to look forward to
  4. 苦くるしい → painful → suffering
Kenshiro's answer is in the next line.

悲しい男よ。誰よりも愛深きゆえに。 N3 誰よりも · N1 ゆえに

Kenshiro · the eulogy for Souther

悲かなしい男おとこよ。誰だれよりも愛深あいふかきゆえに。

Kanashii otoko yo. Dare yori mo ai-fukaki yue ni.

"A sad man… because he loved more deeply than anyone."

Clip
Listen & repeat
Record yourself
Clip: YouTube (same video as line 5, the ending)

Word by word

wordmeaning
悲かなしい男おとこよ"O sad man" — よ after a noun = calling out, poetic
誰だれよりもmore than anyone (誰 + より + も)
愛深あいふかき愛が深い "deep in love" — 深き is the classical form of 深い before a noun
ゆえにbecause of that / therefore — formal, written, dramatic

Grammar: 誰よりも (N3) · 〜ゆえに (N1) · 〜き adjectives

誰よりも + adjective = more than anyone. Swap 誰 for 何 (anything), どこ (anywhere).
Noun / plain form + ゆえに = because of. You will meet it in essays, news, and every villain speech. Everyday speech uses から / ので.
  • 母ははは誰だれよりも優やさしい my mother is kinder than anyone
  • 経験不足けいけんぶそくゆえに失敗しっぱいした I failed because of lack of experience
  • 古ふるき良よき時代じだい the good old days (古い→古き, 良い→良き)
Tokyo use: 「若き日の〜」(in his younger days) on museum captions; 「〜ゆえ」on formal notices.

Drill

  1. 彼かれは速はやい。He is faster than anyone.
  2. 準備不足じゅんびぶそくミスをした。I made a mistake because of poor preparation.
  3. 若わかい → 日ひyoung → younger days (classical)
Look at the sky while you say it.
